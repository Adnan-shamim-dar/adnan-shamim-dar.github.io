01 AI research · artificial life

Genesis

What happens when you give an AI agent something to lose?

Genesis is my artificial-life lab: single-cell agents that can actually die, and no fitness function telling them what to want. Whatever behaviour shows up has to emerge on its own. I run it like a clinical trial, with powered ablations, null baselines and controls.

  • Fear matters. Remove it and agents reproduce about a third less.
  • Honest numbers. A null baseline showed my own headline metric was inflated, so I cut it by 88%.
  • 455 automated tests. A framework paper is in preparation.
  • Emergent behaviour
  • Agent simulation
  • Experimental design

03 Systems engineering · private AI

Tandem

Your own private AI, on hardware you already own. No cloud. No telemetry.

Tandem runs open models locally behind an OpenAI-compatible API. It can pool the memory of your laptop, phone and tablet to run a model too big for any one of them. Every connection is end-to-end encrypted, and each answer shows which device produced it.
